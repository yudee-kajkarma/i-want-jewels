# Spanish (es)

- **Address the reader as `usted`** in instructional copy, but prefer impersonal
  constructions (`se recomienda`, `conviene`) where they read more naturally.
  Never `tú`.
- **UNDECIDED — confirm before the backfill:** this guide assumes Castilian
  Spanish (Spain), using `pendientes` for earrings. Your existing content leans
  Latin American (`arete` appears more often than `pendiente`). This is an SEO
  targeting decision, not a language one — pick the market, then fix this line.
- Opening punctuation is required: `¿Cómo…?`, `¡…!`.
- Decimal comma, thousands point: `1,5 quilates`.
- `joyería` for the category; `joyas` for the pieces themselves.
- Spanish runs ~15% longer than English; shorten meta copy actively.
- Watch gender agreement across the whole noun phrase, not just the adjacent
  adjective.
