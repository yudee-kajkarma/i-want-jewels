# Dutch (nl)

- **Address the reader as `u`.** Never `jij`/`je`. Dutch e-commerce skews
  informal, but this brand is positioned as fine jewellery.
- Compound nouns are written closed: `tennisarmband`, `oorstekers`,
  `geboortesteen` — never spaced. Spaced compounds are the single most common
  Dutch machine-translation error.
- Decimal comma: `1,5 karaat`.
- Dutch runs ~10% longer than English; shorten meta copy where needed.
- Retain English loanwords where the Dutch trade uses them (`ear stack`,
  `statement`, `huggies`, `demi-fine`) rather than forcing a native coinage.
- `sieraden` is the category term; `juwelen` implies higher-end/heirloom.
