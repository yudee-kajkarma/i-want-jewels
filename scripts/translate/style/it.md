# Italian (it)

- **Address the reader as `Lei`** in instructional copy, or use impersonal
  constructions (`si consiglia`). Never `tu`.
- Use `gioielli` for the pieces, `gioielleria` for the category/store.
- Decimal comma: `1,5 carati`.
- Italian runs ~10–15% longer than English; shorten meta copy actively.
- Elide and apostrophise correctly: `l'oro`, `dell'orecchino`, `un'idea`.
- Gender and number agreement must hold across articles, nouns, and adjectives.
